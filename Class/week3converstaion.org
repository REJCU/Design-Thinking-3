*** Chat 1 - Purpose of a sprint pilot 

Insight - the sprint pilot has lower stakes involved in the whole proccess, this allows for teams to be more experimentive in their approach rather than relying on old and trusted method's. 

Despite the lower stakes, it can still be hard for people to experiment with new methods. Ice-breakers and the such can work for getting people out of their comfort room but can be re-hearsed. So changing it to something out of left field like kaoroke, which could be very uncomfortable and will be completely new proccess for some could instill a sense of exploration and broaden people's horizon.
