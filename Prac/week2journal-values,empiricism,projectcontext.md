# 3. AI Conversation Reflection
The key insight would be how agile differently shapes what the final product will look like and how it differs from a plan-based development. 

Agile development allows stakeholders to be more inclined to contribute continuously through the project, it allows for more active contribution to what the final product will look like. This is because of the continuous discussion throughout the sprints, able to cleanly change priorities for when new information comes in. This flexibility allows the final product/solution to be a perfect fit for the stakeholders. 

Have time to review artifacts and view it in a balanced lens. Some approaches taken, recorded and formed into artifacts could be harshly critiqued when they might be a possible answer to a question/problem further along the design process. 

Structure your strategies around the group even if you like a particular way of doing it than an other. An anonomous sharing of ideas could reduce the individual ownership for certain people, reducing the overall quality of the final product/solution.
