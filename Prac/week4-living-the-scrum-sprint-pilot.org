** Knowledge Check – Scrum artefacts and events
*** Why does Scrum place so much emphasis on artefacts and events rather than detailed upfront plans?
Detailed upfront plans are rigid, trying to force a future/product without taking into consideration of the ever-changing circumstances around the users, business and the whole ecosystem. Plans also are more effective for subject experts that may or may not be present, guiding the team with their expertise. 

On the other hand, past artefacts and events emphasise retrospection, allowing teams to more easily respond to new information, changing the product to follow along with the changing context of the industry and stakeholders. 

By understanding and effectively utilising Scrum artifacts and events, teams can create a
rhythm of productive work, regular feedback, and continuous improvement. This leads to better
products, happier customers, and more engaged teams.
